[event] Workspace created — sources: prd-vuongnguyen-2026-06-29/prd.md
[decision] Form factor: MoMo mini app (mobile-first, runs inside MoMo iOS/Android). Also accessible via web.
[assumption] Product has its own visual brand/identity inside the MoMo shell — not just bare MoMo UI
[decision] Emotional target: "official and trustworthy" — not playful, not cold, not startup-casual
[decision] Visual references: MoMo, Grab, Uber, Techcombank — clean fintech/super-app aesthetic, card-based, high-contrast CTAs, minimal decoration
[assumption] Product name: TBD — placeholder needed for DESIGN.md
[decision] Color theme: Navy Authority — primary #1A2E4A, CTA #2563EB, surface white #FFFFFF, light background
[decision] Typography: warm and approachable — slightly rounded letterforms, readable, service-oriented not form-like. Rationale: product is a service, not a government counter.
[decision] Corner radius: soft rounded — cards, buttons, inputs all use generous border-radius (like MoMo/Grab)
[decision] First screen is NOT a form — it is a trust/value pitch. Minh needs to answer: what can this do, can I trust it, why not just use an agency — before he will proceed.
[decision] Landing screen leads with 2 value props: (1) cheaper than agencies, (2) available 24/7 not just 9-5
[assumption] Eligibility gate ("know before you pay") is a product feature but not the primary landing hook — price and availability open the door first
[decision] Core user flow: landing → pick Japan/China → profile questions → eligibility result (pass or hard-to-pass with reason) → travel dates → document checklist
[decision] If something is wrong in profile, system tells Minh clearly what the issue is before he proceeds
[decision] Two pre-payment gates: (1) profile eligibility, (2) travel date feasibility — both must pass before cost is shown and payment is collected
[decision] Payment placement: after both gates pass, before document checklist — not after AI review
[decision] Full flow: landing → destination → profile → eligibility result → travel dates → feasibility check → cost → payment → checklist → upload → AI review → fix loop → submit → status tracking
[decision] Travel date gate rationale: Japan processing 5-9 business days + prep time; if dates too close, block before payment
[decision] Voice and tone: "calm confident authority" — like a doctor saying you're healthy, not a celebration. Warm but not cheerful.
[decision] Pass microcopy: "Hồ sơ của bạn trông tốt ✓" — quiet signal, no congratulations
[decision] Fail microcopy: "Chúng tôi phát hiện một vấn đề ⚠️" + specific reason + "Agencies would still take your money — we won't." — frames fail as protection not rejection. CTA: "Xem cách cải thiện" (how to improve, not dead end)
[decision] Fail state always offers a path forward — never a dead end
[open] Status tracking screen during embassy wait period — minimum: simple timeline (received → submitted → processing → result). Detail TBD.
[decision] Price shown on its own screen after travel dates confirmed — not on eligibility result screen. Sequence: eligibility pass → travel dates → feasibility check → price screen → payment
[decision] CRITICAL CONSTRAINT: Embassy does not publish official bank balance thresholds. Agencies are prohibited from advising applicants on financial tricks (e.g. borrowing money to boost balance). AI must NOT state specific balance minimums or coach financial preparation — this would fabricate embassy policy and violate embassy rules.
[decision] Eligibility gate signals — CAN check: employment type, travel history (prior stamps), prior denial history (when + which country), travel date feasibility, passport validity, document types structurally available by profile. CANNOT check: specific balance thresholds, income sufficiency, or any financial coaching.
[decision] Prior denial is a key eligibility signal: Japan 6-month reapplication ban — if denied within last 6 months, gate blocks before payment. Question framing: "Bạn đã từng bị từ chối visa không?" (Have you ever been denied a visa?)
[change] PRD FR-A4 needs correction — "explicitly states the bank balance minimum threshold" is not valid. Must be removed or reframed as profile-pattern signal only, not a rule.
