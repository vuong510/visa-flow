First Principles Thinking

Why does a visa even exist?

Strip everything back. A visa is a promise: this person is safe, won't harm your citizens, will return home. Perfect proof = strong reason to return (job, family, property) + no legal issues. Everything else is theatre.

safe traveller will return social proof trust signal
🔄
Reverse Brainstorming

How to guarantee failure?

Flip it: send wrong checklists, charge ineligible applicants, give inconsistent advice, never monitor embassy rule changes. Now reverse each failure mode into a product feature. The AI fixes every one of them.

wrong checklist → fixed ineligible charged → gate blocks embassy drift → auto-monitor
💡
Assumption Reversal

Open to everyone? No — only the best.

"We serve all applicants" → Flip: only accept high-confidence profiles. Selective, not open-to-all. This is a moat, not a limitation. When you say no, people trust your yes even more.

selective intake confidence filter trust through scarcity
🔪
Friction Autopsy (invented)

Where does the user actually bleed?

Dissect every step where users drop off or suffer. Steps 1–4: price check, soft eligibility, document checklist, document review — every single one is AI-replaceable. Post-submission: total black box. That's where they bleed most.

4 steps → AI black box pain status visibility
❤️
Empathy Map

What's in their head at 11pm?

"Where do I start?" "My profile won't pass." "Is this agency even real?" Fear drives every decision. They don't want information — they want belief. The product's job is to make them believe they will pass.

"Will I pass?" fear of rejection trust signal belief engine
🎭
Role Playing

Play the firm owner. What's their first question?

"Can we fucking trust AI?" The firm owner needs proof — a complete conversation log and one real approved case. Not a demo. Not a pitch deck. A passport with a Japan stamp issued to a real person after AI consultation.

owner skepticism proof = real case complete transcript

TRIZ Contradiction

Faster AND more accurate?

Judgment = conditional rules by profile type. This resolves the contradiction — AI applies rules consistently at speed 1000x a human. The industry doesn't know AI scope is this large. That gap is the unfair advantage.

conditional rules profile-typed logic unfair advantage

Key Insights

The things that matter.

Not brainstorm outputs — these are the load-bearing ideas the whole product rests on.

"Trust IS the product. Every feature is either building trust or it doesn't belong."
Core Insight — Emotional Job
01
Emotional Core
Eligibility gate IS the trust moment
The screen where AI says "yes, your profile qualifies" is where the user stops being afraid. Not the approval. Not the stamp. This screen. Design it like the most important moment in the product.
02
Go-to-Market
One successful case is the foundation of everything
Firm owner trust, user trust, word-of-mouth, media — all of it starts when one person's Japan visa gets approved through VisaFlow AI. That case is a passport with a stamp. Ship for that.
03
Business Model
B2B2C — be MoMo, not the bank
Own the consumer brand. Partner with one of the 12 authorized Japan agencies as invisible licensed backend. You are MoMo. The agency is VietcomBank. Users never need to know.
04
AI Scope
Judgment = conditional rules
The industry thinks visa judgment requires human expertise. Wrong. It's conditional rule logic keyed on profile type (student / employee / freelancer). AI scope is far larger than anyone assumes. This is the unfair advantage.
05
Cost Structure
AI replaces the biggest cost → price disruption
Staff salaries are ~70% of a traditional agency's cost base. Replace staff with AI. Pass savings to users. Undercut legacy agencies while making more margin. New segment: young travellers who couldn't afford agencies before.
06
Constraint
12 authorized agencies — partner with one
Only ~12 agencies can physically submit to Japan embassy. This is not a threat — it's a moat enabler. Partner with one, and no one can replicate the B2B2C model without a partner of their own. Scarcity creates barriers to entry.
07
AI Transparency
Never hide the AI — full transparency always
Users know they're talking to AI from the first message. This isn't a weakness — it's positioning. Agencies pretend to be human. You own the honest brand. In a market built on fear, honesty is radical.
08
Out of Scope
Quota rejections are not your problem
Two rejection types: profile-based (AI can fix) and structural (quota, policy — nobody can fix). Be clear with users: when the embassy runs out of slots, no one can help. Accepting this is a product boundary, not a failure.
